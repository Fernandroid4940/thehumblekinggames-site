PRIVACY POLICY

Effective November 2026 · Applies to 4th & Glory for iPhone

4th & Glory ("the game") is published by The Humble King Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

  • We don't collect any information from you.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or in-app purchases.
  • Your career is saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account.

Your saved game

Your career (team, players, results and settings) is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, a copy is also stored in your personal iCloud account by Apple, so your career can follow you to another device. We can't see or access either copy. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Beta testing

If you test the game through TestFlight, Apple may share information with us, such as your name and email if you were invited directly, your device model and iOS version, how often you open the game, crash reports, and any screenshots or feedback you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve the game. Apple's practices are described in its privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't run our own servers for the game, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share personal information.

Children

We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can turn off iCloud for the game in iOS Settings. Deleting the game removes the copy on your iPhone. You can remove the iCloud copy from your iCloud storage settings. To ask about beta feedback you sent us, or to have it deleted, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
