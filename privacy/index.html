PRIVACY POLICY

Effective December 2026 · Applies to Nine to Jackpot for iPhone

Nine to Jackpot ("the game") is published by The Humble King Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • Optional reward ads are shown by Google AdMob, which collects device and usage data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (cash, jobs, outfits, characters, settings and purchase records) is saved on your iPhone. We don't receive it. Deleting the game deletes it, unless you exported a save code.

Advertising

The game offers optional ads you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. These are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may collect:

DataUsed for
Device identifiers (the Advertising Identifier, only if you allow tracking, and an app-specific identifier)Showing ads, limiting repeats, measuring results and preventing fraud
IP address and approximate location (country or region)Showing relevant ads and complying with local laws
Ad interactions and app usage (for example, whether an ad was watched)Measuring ads and confirming rewards
Diagnostics (crash and performance data)Keeping ads working

The first time you watch an ad, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized using your Advertising Identifier. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are also asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change these choices at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered. We never receive your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

We don't run our own analytics, we don't sell personal information, and we don't share data with anyone other than the ad and payment providers described above.

Play money only

All casino games use in-game play money. No real money or prizes can be won, and nothing in the game can be exchanged for real money.

Children

The game contains simulated gambling and is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can reset or limit your Advertising Identifier in iOS Settings, decline tracking, or delete the game at any time. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are handled by Google; email us and we'll help you with the process.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
