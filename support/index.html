SUPPORT

Need help with Nine to Jackpot? Email fernando.lopez@thehumblekinggames.com and include your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

I bought something but didn't get it

Open the Shop tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases. This brings back one-time purchases on the same Apple ID. Dollar Bills packs can't be restored once used. If a purchase still didn't arrive, email us with the date and the item name.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Go to the Shop tab, scroll to Settings and tap Export save. Copy the save code, then on the new phone tap Import save and paste it.

Can I win real money?

No. Every casino game uses play money. Nothing in the game can be exchanged for real money or prizes.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. You can turn off personalized ads at any time in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
